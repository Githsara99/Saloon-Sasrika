Our Story

A quiet studio, an unhurried chair

Sashrika Bridal Salon was built around one idea: your wedding morning should feel calm, not rushed. We keep our bookings small so every bride gets the full attention of our artists — from the first trial to the final pin in your hair.

We work across bridal, homecoming and photoshoot looks, blending traditional Kandyan and Indian bridal artistry with softer, modern finishes — whatever suits the day you're building.

  • Complimentary trial before the big day
  • Long-wear makeup suited to Sri Lankan weather
  • In-studio or home & hotel visits
